Who we are

A facilitation desk that also operates.

ZED Services works where Chinese supply meets West African market reality. We help entrepreneurs import and build, and we support organisations that need reliable eyes on the ground. Because we run real estate and China–Côte d'Ivoire trade operations ourselves, we understand the projects we work on from the inside.

  • Sourcing & Import Management Primary

    Purchasing coaching in China, introductions to reliable established suppliers, quality inspection and container groupage to Abidjan.

  • Real Estate – Côte d'Ivoire

    Land acquisition support, project delivery and property management. We run these projects ourselves.

  • China–Côte d'Ivoire Trade

    Sourcing, logistics and supplier relations along the corridor. Active supplier network in Guangzhou, Foshan and Yiwu.

  • Project Structuring

    Business plans, financial models and project files, built to institutional standard.

China–Côte d'Ivoire Trade

Purchasing coaching in China: we connect buyers with reliable, well-established suppliers in Guangzhou, Foshan and Yiwu and guide them through selection, negotiation and quality control. We also manage container groupage, consolidating goods from several buyers into shared containers shipped back to Côte d'Ivoire.
